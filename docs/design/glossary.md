# Glossary

| Term | Definition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | The business that holds the WEX relationship. A customer has one or more accounts. | Decided |
| Account | The unit that holds one credit line, one currency and one statement. A customer can split accounts by legal entity, region or another way. The account switcher changes the current account. | Decided |
| All accounts | The switcher option for admins with more than one account. It shows rolled-up dashboard, spend, rebates and reports. Actions still happen inside one account. | Decided |
| Department | A label on cards and users within an account, used for filtering, reporting and approvals. One level only, not a tree. | Decided |
| Admin | A user who manages cards, people, payments, and settings for an account. | Proposed |
| Card and payment manager | A user who handles daily operations: issuing cards, paying suppliers, and resolving exceptions. | Proposed |
| Cardholder | A user who has been issued one or more cards and can see their own cards and transactions. | Proposed |
| Virtual card | A card number issued for online or one-time spend, with no plastic card. | Proposed |
| Physical card | A plastic card sent to a cardholder. | Proposed |
| Rebate | Money returned to the customer based on card spend. | Proposed |
